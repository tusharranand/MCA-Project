Client Background

IntelliServe India operates a rapidly growing digital service platform used by customers across multiple regions. Its digital platform generates customer profile, engagement, service, transaction and satisfaction data. Growth has increased churn-management complexity, and current rule-based reporting does not provide sufficiently early or individualized risk signals for retention teams.

Business Problem

The organisation has substantial customer, usage, support and transaction data but cannot reliably identify which customers are likely to churn. It needs an AI/ML solution that transforms historical data into churn-risk predictions, interpretable drivers and actionable retention priorities while controlling bias, leakage and model risk.

Company Aspiration

Develop an explainable machine-learning solution that predicts customer churn risk, identifies the strongest behavioural drivers, supports targeted retention decisions, and defines a practical path for responsible model deployment and monitoring.
